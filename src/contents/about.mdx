## Taranga – The Wave of Technology

Taranga is the biggest technical festival of **IOE Purwanchal Campus**, created through the collaboration of **ACES** and **EXCESS**. Together, they form one powerful wave that unites creativity, innovation, and collaboration.

## What Makes Taranga Unique

- A platform for students to showcase ideas, research, and innovations
- Hands-on workshops and competitions across hardware, software, AI/ML, and IoT
- Pre-events like learning challenges, AI art, hackathons, and tech talks
- Main events including Hardware & Software Hackathons, AR Bug Hunt, InternFest, and more

## Our Vision

Taranga represents the continuous flow of knowledge and innovation. Just like waves grow stronger when they merge, Taranga builds a legacy of growth, creativity, and collaboration that will continue for years to come.
